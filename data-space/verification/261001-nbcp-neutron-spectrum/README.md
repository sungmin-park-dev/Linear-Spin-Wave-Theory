## Reading

1. The toolkit now gives intensities, not only band positions, so a comparison with the measured spectra of Woodland et al. needs only their data, the lattice constant and the instrument resolution.
2. The middle and right panels show that the lowest three-sublattice branch near Γ carries little weight at Q_z = 1.5/a. Which branches are visible therefore depends on the scattering geometry, which a band-only comparison cannot show.
3. Γ at Q_z = 0 is Q = 0, where the polarization factor is undefined; it is blank in the map.
4. The longitudinal two-magnon continuum (O(S⁰)) is not included (D26).
