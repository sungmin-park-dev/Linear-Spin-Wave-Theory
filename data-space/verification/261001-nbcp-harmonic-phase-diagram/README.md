# NBCP harmonic phase diagram (2026-10-01)

Same two planes as arXiv:2601.20963 Fig. 1 (iDMRG, W = 6): (h, J_PD) at J_Γ = 0 and (h, J_Γ) at J_PD = 0, with J = 0.075, J_z = 0.125 meV, S = 1/2.

Method (pd_scan.py): classical minimum on the 1-, 2-, 3- and 4-site (2×2) cells (differential evolution + continuation in h), LSWT stability on a matched mesh (18 primitive momenta per direction), ranking by E_cl + E_zp among LSWT-stable states. The 3-site state is degenerate along the global-z orbit, so 12 angles in [0, π/3) are tested and the zero-point-selected stable angle is used. Engine: `spintoolkit.methods.phase_competition` from the roadmap thread's D42 patch (/mnt/project-files/roadmap/patches/), not yet on main. Hatched cells: the lowest classical state is LSWT-unstable, so the true state is a modulation outside these cells (undetermined). h = 0 is omitted (degenerate). Grid: h step 0.02 meV; J step 2.5 μeV to 0.03 meV, then 5 μeV (J_Γ only).

Sign symmetry (checked 2026-10-01): the XXZ part and the field along z are invariant under any global spin rotation R_z(α). The PD term carries spin-rotation charge ±2 and the Γ term ±1, so R_z(π/2) maps J_PD → −J_PD (Γ would pick up a phase, not a sign) and R_z(π) maps J_Γ → −J_Γ (PD unchanged). Checked on the bond matrices (R^T J(−) R = J(+) to 4e-18), and on classical energy, LSWT zero-point energy and Q of rotated states (Y, V, stripe, SkX; differences ≤ 1e-17 meV; independent searches at −J give the same energy). A proper rotation about z keeps Q. So each axis needs only J > 0. In the mixed plane only J_Γ → −J_Γ is removable this way. J_PD axis extended to 0.10 meV: 2-site stripe everywhere above 0.03 meV.

Findings
- (h, J_PD): Y stays the ground state over its whole field range up to J_PD ≈ 0.015 meV, is LSWT-unstable at 0.0175–0.02, and is replaced by a 2-site stripe from ≈ 0.0225. V shrinks from the high-field side: unstable at h ≥ 0.46 already at J_PD = 0.005; at 0.010 only h ≈ 0.32–0.38 meV is V (0.40–0.42 unstable, 0.44–0.46 a Q = 0 4-site state); from 0.0125 a 2-site stripe replaces V above h ≈ 0.36. UUD survives to 0.03. No SkX on this axis.
- (h, J_Γ): Y/UUD/V unchanged up to J_Γ ≈ 0.03 meV. Low-field 2-site stripe from J_Γ ≈ 0.03. A 4-site |Q| = 2 SkX (Berg–Lüscher integer, LSWT-stable) takes over from J_Γ ≈ 0.035 (low-field Y side) and ≈ 0.045–0.05 (UUD and lower V). Classical and harmonic boundaries differ by ~5 μeV in J_Γ (zero-point favours UUD). Q is +2 here (field +z); the roadmap's low-field pocket (J_PD ≥ 0.02, J_Γ 0.01–0.02, h ≤ 0.1) is −2, reproduced. Both signs are coupling-selected, not a degenerate pair: at J_PD=0, J_Γ=0.05, h=0.2 the S^y→−S^y reflected texture is 21 μeV/spin higher and relaxes back to +2. So these are two distinct SkX textures.
- Paper Fig. 4 points (J = 0.010 meV): Y at 0.2 T is deep inside Y on both axes. V at 1.4 T (h = 0.376 meV) is inside V on the J_Γ axis but at the edge on the J_PD axis (unstable at h = 0.40; stripe at 1.4 T from J_PD ≈ 0.0125).
- Qualitative agreement with the paper's text: J_PD → 2-site stripe (UCS); J_Γ → stripe from Y at low field and 4-site SkX from UUD/V. Ψ (iDMRG) does not appear at h > 0 at this order.

Limits: harmonic order only; cells ≤ 4 sites; hatched regions need larger/incommensurate cells; paper's Fig. 1 boundary values were not readable here, so comparison is qualitative.
