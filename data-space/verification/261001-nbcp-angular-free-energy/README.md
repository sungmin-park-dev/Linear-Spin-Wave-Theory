## Reading

In the Y state the thermal occupation of the soft (pseudo-Goldstone) branch lowers the sixfold pinning by about 30 % at 0.01 meV ≈ 0.12 K. With the cutoff 0.25 the reduction is about 20 %. With the cutoff 1.0 the remaining modes raise it instead. So the sign of the thermal correction to the pinning depends on how the soft branch is split between the matching and the phase theory. This is the cutoff dependence that Section 7 of the NBCP note asks to record.

These are harmonic results. They are not a finite-temperature phase diagram, and they do not replace the clock-RG matching.
