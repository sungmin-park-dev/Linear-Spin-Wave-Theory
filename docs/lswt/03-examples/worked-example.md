# Worked Example

The example concerns a quadratic boson Hamiltonian and its Bogoliubov transformation. Its scope connects the Hamiltonian coefficients, the positive-definite condition, and the resulting normal modes.

## Topics

- Specification of the quadratic Hamiltonian.
- Bogoliubov transformation.
- Positive-definite condition and the domain of the solution.
