# Paraunitary Diagonalization

The diagonalization of a quadratic bosonic Hamiltonian relates the Nambu operators to magnon modes through a Bogoliubov transformation. The scope includes the paraunitary condition, the Colpa construction, and the conditions under which that construction applies.

## Topics

- Bogoliubov transformation and preservation of the Nambu metric.
- Colpa diagonalization for a positive-definite quadratic form.
- Applicability limits and the separate treatment required for Goldstone modes.
