# Thermodynamics

The thermodynamic description uses the magnon energies and occupations to determine equilibrium quantities. Energy, temperature, entropy, and system-size symbols follow [Notation and Conventions](../00-foundations/notation-and-conventions.md).

## Quantities

- Partition function.
- Internal and free energies.
- Entropy and specific heat.
- Boson number and spin moment obtained from correlation matrices, in connection with [Magnon Observables](magnon-observables.md).

## References

### Internal Documents

- [Magnon Observables](magnon-observables.md): supplies the magnon energies and occupations used by the thermodynamic expressions.
- [Thermodynamic Derivations](../04-appendices/thermodynamic-derivations.md): is reserved for the longer entropy and correlation-matrix derivations.
- [Notation and Conventions](../00-foundations/notation-and-conventions.md): fixes the energy, temperature, entropy, and system-size symbols.
