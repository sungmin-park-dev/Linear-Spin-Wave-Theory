# Magnon Observables

The magnon spectrum and Bogoliubov transformation connect the diagonalized Hamiltonian to its observable properties. The quantities considered here provide the inputs for thermodynamics, spin correlations, and topological response.

## Quantities

- Magnon energies and band structure.
- Zero-point energy correction.
- Boson occupation.
- Correlation matrices after diagonalization.
