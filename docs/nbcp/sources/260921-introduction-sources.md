# Introduction source record

Scope: the user requested a concise Introduction of at most one page covering
recent spin-supersolid studies, related materials, the present research scope,
and an NBCP structure illustration. This is a literature-based revision;
physics acceptance and the existing calculations are unchanged. Source pages
were checked on 2026-09-21. The material table is representative, not an
exhaustive review. The July 2026 result is explicitly identified as a preprint.

| Source | Role and checked content |
|---|---|
| [Gao et al., npj Quantum Materials 7, 89 (2022)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41535-022-00500-3) | Article model discussion: triangular Co layers, effective doublet, thermodynamic model fit. Figure 1(a) and its caption; Rights and permissions. |
| [Giant magnetocaloric effect in spin supersolid candidate NBCP, Nature 625, 270–275 (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06885-w) | Abstract: cooling and neutron-diffraction evidence. These are reported signatures, not a proof of dissipationless spin transport. |
| [NMR study of spin supersolid phases in NBCP, Physical Review B 112, 125163 (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1103/gsk8-1k9q) | Abstract: Y/UUD/V identification and low-energy excitation evidence. |
| [Phase diagram and spectroscopic signatures of a supersolid in K2Co(SeO3)2, Nature Communications (18 February 2026)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-69661-0) | Abstract: translation breaking, low-energy neutron modes and low/high-field phases. |
| [Transverse order and longitudinal fluctuations in a near-Ising spin supersolid, arXiv:2607.13635v2 (28 July 2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13635v2) | Abstract and version record: transverse ordered component from neutron scattering; retained as a preprint. |
| [Spin-supersolidity induced quantum criticality and magnetocaloric effect in Rb2Co(SeO3)2 (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41535-026-00881-9) | Abstract: high-field NMR and thermodynamic evidence consistent with a supersolid. |
| [Park et al., arXiv:2601.20963v1 (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.20963v1) | Abstract: SOC-induced gaps, claimed thermal stabilization and skyrmion candidate. Claims are attributed to that paper; this note does not claim to have completed its iDMRG or thermal analysis. |
