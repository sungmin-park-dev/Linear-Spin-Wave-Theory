\section{Skyrmion Phase}\label{sec-nbcp-skyrmion}

The four-site SkX order reported by the target paper is a candidate
competitor, not a phase independently established by the present
calculations. The source notes contain a four-sublattice variational
construction, but its stationary texture, stability and signed
topological charge require a consistent audit before it can be used in a
phase comparison.

\subsection{Spin texture and topological
identification}\label{spin-texture-and-topological-identification}

For a four-site noncoplanar texture, scalar chirality and geometric
topological charge are different diagnostics. The signed triple product
\(\mathbf n_i\cdot(\mathbf n_j\times\mathbf n_k)\) is an oriented local
chirality. A lattice solid angle additionally depends on dot products
and a branch choice. The original four-site draft takes an absolute
value in its solid-angle numerator; that loses orientation and cannot be
used to establish a signed skyrmion charge. No topological charge from
that draft is adopted in the present phase classification.

\subsection{Energy and stability
requirements}\label{energy-and-stability-requirements}

A completed characterization must specify the periodic spin texture, the
orientation and counting of elementary triangles, the signed
topological-charge convention and the energy per physical spin.
Comparison with Y, V and stripe states must use the same Hamiltonian
parameters. A magnon spectrum would test local stability of the selected
texture; it would not replace that energy comparison. These calculations
and a verified SkX band structure are not yet supplied here.
